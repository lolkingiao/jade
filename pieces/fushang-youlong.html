專業鑑賞筆記

Notes on material and hand

種質與沁色

此件取料厚大，選用地方白玉。最引人注目的是其深邃的水銀滲（黑沁），沁色深入肌理，色澤濃郁而不死板，展現出古玉「入土千年」的神韻。玉質表面具極佳的熟糯感與自然的「胞漿」，觸手溫涼潤澤。

工藝與造型

造型具備典型的戰漢時期特徵，威嚴大氣。採「滿工」手法，無論正面或背面均雕琢細膩，無一處留白。祥龍結構精準，刀法簡練卻神態盡顯。搭配之黑檀木珠質地深沉，與水銀沁色相呼應，更添物件的肅穆與雅致。

Stone and staining

Cut from a thick block of regional white jade. The mercury-black staining penetrates the grain — rich but never deadened: the 'thousand years in the earth' quality of archaic jade. The surface has a ripe, glutinous feel and a natural patina.

Carving and form

Classic Warring-States and Han bearing: austere and powerful. Worked 滿工 — no blank ground on either face. The dragon's structure is precise; the cutting is economical yet fully expressive. The ebony bead echoes the black staining and adds a grave elegance.

文化寓意與用途

What the subject wishes

寓意　「府上有龍」寓意家族顯赫、貴人守護、府邸平安。龍在戰漢時期不僅是皇權與神性的象徵，更具備強大的避邪化煞、守護宅邸之祈願。

用途　體積碩大扎實，極適合資深收藏家作為手把件盤玩感悟，或作為居家、書房之重點案頭陳設。

府上有龍 — 'a dragon in the mansion' — speaks of an illustrious house, protection by noble spirits, and peace within the household. In the Warring-States and Han imagination the dragon was both imperial and apotropaic, warding off evil.

Large and solid, it suits a seasoned collector's hand or a place of emphasis in a study or hall.
