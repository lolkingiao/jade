專業鑑賞筆記

Notes on material and hand

種質與玉色

此件取料厚重，選用經典青黃玉。玉質結構綿密，經長年盤玩後，「脂潤感」極強。玉色青中帶黃，過渡自然，部分區域帶有歲月沁色，愈發顯得古香古色。

工藝與造型

工藝具明顯明代特徵，刀法剛勁有力，不拘小節而神韻十足。盤龍造型結構嚴謹，龍鱗與龍鬚刻畫流暢。鏤空與圓雕結合之處打磨圓潤，上手把玩手感紮實，層次感分明。搭配之巴西紅瑪瑙色澤濃郁，點綴於青黃玉間，增添視覺亮點與富貴之氣。

Stone and colour

Cut from a thick block of classic celadon-yellow jade, tightly structured and, after long handling, exceptionally fatty. The colour moves from green to yellow naturally, with areas of age-staining that deepen its archaic tone.

Carving and form

Markedly Ming: the cutting is firm and unhurried, careless of trifles but full of spirit. The coiled dragon is rigorously constructed; scales and whiskers flow. Where openwork meets round carving the finish is smooth, and it has a satisfying weight in the hand.

文化寓意與用途

What the subject wishes

寓意　龍為中華瑞獸之首，象徵「至高無上、尊貴祥瑞、守護家宅」。「盤龍」更有基業穩固、聚氣納財之意。

用途　極適合高級收藏、案頭擺設（書鎮），或作為日常盤玩之大型手把件，感悟古玉靈性。

The dragon heads all auspicious beasts — supreme, noble, protective of the household. A coiled dragon further suggests a firmly established foundation and the gathering of wealth.

For advanced collecting, for a desk as a paperweight, or as a large daily companion piece.

Stated plainly

品相　此為古董玉器，整體保存狀態良好，結構完整。玉面留有天然石紋與時代沁色痕跡，屬古玉鑑定之重要依據，不影響整體美觀與藝術價值。

承諾　本人曾從事珠寶行業，保證所有描述真實、誠信。誠摯歡迎預約親自鑑賞，感受明代古玉之雄渾氣息。

Condition　Well preserved and structurally complete. Natural stone lines and period staining are the evidence of old jade and do not affect its appearance or value.
